## Added on 2026-10-13

* Editorial review queue (admin → תור בדיקה) for flagged texts, mappings and derived links; decisions are
  audited and **survive re-imports** (previously a re-import reset admin verdicts and the YouTube-title flag).
* Shared rate limiting across instances (`RATE_LIMIT_STORE=postgres`, migration `20261013000000_rate_limit_counters`),
  verified with 30 concurrent requests from two connection pools against a limit of 10 (exactly 10 allowed) and
  on the production build (6 × 200, then 429).
* PNG app icons (192, 512, maskable 512, apple-touch 180) generated from the SVGs.
* Arabic stop words are now compared after normalisation (e.g. "على" no longer counts as a search term).

## Added on 2026-10-13 (Hebrew videos and strict language)

* Hebrew workbook imported (1,061 mappings from 8 series sheets, educator + parent), see SOURCE_AUDIT §11.
* Strict display language: no cross-language video fallback; video language from caption track or workbook column only.
* Grounding re-scored on the milestone's own passages; fixed not-covered message per language; quoted evidence
  with page; `source_unavailable` for an unindexed handbook; unavailable PDF link reported.
* Eval: milestone top-1 95.1 % in scope, near-domain questions refused 7/7, off-topic refused, answer and
  citation language 100 %. Two in-scope questions (he-08, ar-01) are refused rather than answered — a recall
  gap left in place rather than loosening the thresholds. The Arabic home-screen suggestion that hit this gap
  was rephrased.

## Pending / not verified

* **Claude answers not exercised**: no `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` was available, so all runs used the extractive
  mode. The integration compiles against the SDK and is guarded (validated codes, refusal handling), but its
  answer quality is untested.
* **Hebrew video language**: 1,042 of 1,058 Hebrew videos rely on the workbook column; the caption-track check
  was stopped by YouTube's rate limit (not bypassed). 54 contradicting and 73 needs-review Hebrew mappings
  await an editor.
* **English** (or any language other than he/ar) is not supported: there are no English sources; the API rejects it.
* **Editorial review** still has to be *done by people* (the tooling now exists): 5 flagged milestone
  translations (2.1 ×2, 3.2 ×2, 2.7 he), 5 mappings, 196 derived links, the 6 Arabic-specific exercises of 2.1,
  the scale-chart row transcription, and the video-language labels (from metadata, not from listening).
* Not built, deliberately: push notifications (no use case in the specification) and offline *answering*
  (retrieval needs the server; browsing pages already work offline once visited).
